This independent proof of concept is developed with AI assistance. I review and learn the implementation, validate analytical claims, and document decisions and remaining limitations. It is a work in progress and is not affiliated with NABLA Mobility.

The initial audit/tests/README predated this session; exact authorship was not established. The user supplied the problem, scope, preferred stack, evidence limits, transparency requirements and public publishing authorization.

AI researched sources, modified/generated implementation, tests, reports, CI and Docker; ran checks, interpreted results provisionally and handled authorized publication. Agent checks are execution evidence, not independent peer review or personal comprehension. Unchecked AI output is unverified; passing tests cannot establish aviation suitability.
